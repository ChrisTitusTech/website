## Text Messaging Privacy

The Chris Titus Tech text messaging program includes order updates, customer service communications, and marketing messages. When you opt in, we collect your mobile number and your opt-in and consent information to send the message types you agree to receive and manage your messaging preferences. Consent to receive text messages is separate from using our website or accepting this Privacy Policy.

No phone or mobile number information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are excluded from all other data-sharing categories in this policy. Your SMS messaging data and consent will not be shared with non-affiliated third parties for their own use; explicit one-to-one consent to receive messages from Chris Titus Tech does not authorize messages from another brand.

We do not sell or rent SMS opt-in information or consent. We share this information only as necessary to deliver the specific services associated with the messaging program. This limited sharing may include your mobile number and SMS opt-in or consent status with messaging platform providers, phone carriers, and other vendors involved in delivering text messages on our behalf. These providers may use the information only to provide those services, not for their own marketing or any non-service-related purpose.

You can opt out by replying **STOP** to a text message or contacting [contact@christitus.com](mailto:contact@christitus.com). Reply **HELP** for assistance. See our [Text Messaging Terms and Conditions](/terms-conditions/#text-messaging-terms-and-conditions) for message frequency, rates, and additional program details.
